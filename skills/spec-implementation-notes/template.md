## Status Legend

- `open` — question raised, no decision yet
- `accepted` — assumption in force, implementation reflects it
- `changed` — previously accepted decision overturned (a follow-up entry records the new state)
- `resolved` — confirmed by user or evidence, no further change expected

Never delete entries. Reversed decisions transition through `changed` and gain a follow-up entry — the audit trail is the point.
